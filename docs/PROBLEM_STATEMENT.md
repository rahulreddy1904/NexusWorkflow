# NEXUS Workflow — Problem Statement

## The Challenge

Modern organizations struggle with recurring operational requests—such as IT incident triage, vendor compliance reviews, billing disputes, customer escalations, and employee onboarding. Today, these operations suffer from four critical systemic failure modes:

1. **Fragmented Tooling & Context Loss**: Operational teams bounce between siloed ticketing queues, chat channels, monitoring dashboards, spreadsheets, and internal databases. Critical context, timestamps, and reasoning get lost across handoffs.
2. **Repetitive Decision Latency**: Highly compensated operational specialists waste hours manually gathering telemetry, cross-referencing policy documents, verifying checklists, and routing requests before taking any consequential action.
3. **Inconsistent & Unauditable Execution**: Different operators follow varying ad-hoc procedures. There is no unified, immutable ledger documenting why a triage decision was made, what evidence was evaluated, or which checklist items were validated.
4. **All-or-Nothing Automation Risks**: Traditional automation scripts either fail on edge cases or execute blindly without governance. Without granular human-in-the-loop (HITL) checkpoints, automated scripts risk executing high-impact, irreversible mutations (e.g., unauthorized refunds, service restarts, elevated permission grants) without human oversight.

## Target Impact

NEXUS Workflow eliminates this friction by unifying operational request intake and autonomous multi-agent AI execution into a single, observable, governed workspace. Organizations maintain full human oversight over high-risk actions while delegating multi-step planning, telemetry analysis, checklist synthesis, and task execution to autonomous AI agents.
